How to Make "Snack" Letter Snacks

What you will need

  • 384g flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 450ml of milk

Equipment you will need

  • A bowl
  • A whisk
  • An oven
  • A plate
  • A jug

Instructions

  1. Pour 315g of flour into the bowl. The remaining 69g will be used for later.
  2. Crack open 2 eggs and pour its contents into the bowl. the remaining egg will be used for later.
  3. Pour 202ml of milk into a jug, then pour the milk from the jug into the bowl. The remaining 248ml will be used for later.
  4. Mix mix mix... til you get something that looks like batter.
  5. Take small parts of the batter and shape them into letters that look like "S", "n", "a", "c" and "k"
  6. Put the cookies in the oven and bake at 180 degrees Celsius for 30 minutes.
  7. After 30 minutes, take the cookies out of the oven and enjoy eating the abomination

BONUS TIP(S)! If you want, you can use your remaining ingredients to decorate your "Snack" snacks! And if you happen to own a food company, you may also want to put them all into a cardboard box and sell your snacks for money!
